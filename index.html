Semi-Finished Goods / WIP Definition

Garments or components that have entered production but are not yet QC-passed finished goods. Track cutting completed, parts bundled, embroidery / logo work, pocket or collar work, stitching in progress, alteration and rework separately where your workflow requires it.

Quantity Reconciliation

Pending Qty = Sale Order Qty − QC Passed / Finished Goods Qty. WIP is a stage-wise subset of pending quantity; do not add WIP again to the pending quantity. Rejected / rework pieces remain pending until passed and received into FG.
